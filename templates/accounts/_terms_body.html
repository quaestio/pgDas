1. What PGDAS is

PGDAS is a web tool for looking at and administering PostgreSQL servers: browsing, running SQL, changing data and structure, backups and monitoring. Registering an organization gives its first Super User the right to add people, connect servers and decide what each person may do.

2. Accounts and organizations

  • An account belongs to one person and is identified by one email address, which cannot be used by anyone else on this installation. Do not share your password.
  • The first Super User of an organization is responsible for every person they add, for the permissions they give and for the PostgreSQL logins they connect.
  • Only register an organization that you are authorised to manage, with details that are true.

3. Acceptable use

  • Use PGDAS only on databases you are allowed to administer or query.
  • Do not try to bypass restrictions (permissions, address restrictions, review popups), to reach another organization's data, or to disturb the service for others.
  • Report weaknesses you find to the operator instead of using them.

4. Changes you make are real

Changes made through PGDAS act directly on the databases you connect. PGDAS asks for confirmation and keeps records, but it cannot guarantee that every change can be undone. Keep backups, and connect PostgreSQL with logins that have only the privileges that are needed.

5. Records

PGDAS records sign-ins, the address they come from, statements run, changes made and refused attempts in an audit log kept by the operator. The people of an organization are told this and agree to it by using PGDAS.

6. Credentials and data
